Version 3. The empirical scale Θ_c = (2πΦ(a)/(2πc − 9/2))⁴ is replaced by the Fuchs scale d_c = (2¹⁴/3)√2 π⁵ c^{9/2} e^{−4πc}, that is, by the asymptotics of the defect of the prolate spheroidal function with index 4, and the quantity κ_Θ by the quantity κ_d = −¼ d ln d_c/da = 2πc − 9/4. The scale Θ_c behaves as c⁵e^{−4πc}, and the defect as c^{9/2}e^{−4πc}. Therefore the numerical remark of version 2 that the computed infimum coincides with Θ_c up to a factor of order unity is replaced by a comparison with d_c. Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 are unchanged. In Theorem 1.3 the quantities Θ_c and κ_Θ are replaced by d_c and κ_d, while the proof remains the same. Lemmas 5.3–5.5 on the scale are proved directly from the definition of d_c. In Corollary 5.8 the factor C_Θ^{⌈D_k⌉} is replaced by e^{−4πD_k}, and the bound 1 + 1.07γ by 1 + γ. The numerical statements in units of the scale are recalculated: the rigorous bound on the mean boundary balance over [5, 71] is 1.0009 instead of 1.0022, the constant of Corollary 5.7(c) is 0.950 instead of 1.973, and in the proof of Proposition 8.16 the constant value θ_k is 8 instead of 2. Figures 2–4 and the tables of Appendix A are rebuilt. In addition, the formulations of a number of statements and proofs have been refined, and a comparison with the work of Andrews, Connes and La Gatta (arXiv:2611.13129) and references to the author's works on the logarithmic Laplacian have been added.
